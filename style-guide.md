# Version B — Clear English

English-first website with Hungarian support labels. Navy #11233b, yellow #ffdc36, white and pale grey #f7f9fc. Arial/Helvetica keeps this version familiar and readable.

## Hierarchy
One large question in the hero, followed by four rectangular service links: Reading, Writing, Pronunciation, Translation. English labels are bold and Hungarian labels secondary. Each link scrolls to its matching service card.

Service cards use a yellow top border, number badge, strong title, comfortable padding and short copy. Body text stays at least 16px. Mobile cards stack; the four hero links form a two-by-two grid.

## Character and photography
Original monochrome gentleman SVG: smiling, clean-shaven, suit, no hat. It is a temporary brand character, not a portrait. The character appears beside Mark's introduction.

The supplied photo is displayed inside a 720:384 crop window, hiding the colour bars without modifying the original file. Caption: “Look for my profile picture on LinkedIn and Instagram.”
